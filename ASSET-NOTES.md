# Aset ukiran belakang pintu v2.2

Aset proyek: `public/assets/door-back-v22.webp`.

Dibuat dengan Imagegen bawaan memakai gambar gerbang sebagai referensi bahan dan gaya. Hasil dikonversi ke WebP untuk aplikasi. Tekstur hanya memuat ukiran, lis, roset, dan engsel, tanpa teks tertanam. Cincin dan aksen sudut ditambahkan sebagai geometri oleh `public/palace.js`.

Prompt aset:

Create a production texture for the BACK surfaces of two Javanese royal teak door leaves. Use the existing gate only as a material and craftsmanship reference. Orthographic straight-on close-up, two rectangular leaf backs side by side, filling a portrait canvas in a 380:437 width-to-height ratio. Dark polished chocolate teak, deeply carved Jepara lotus and botanical arabesques, antique 24k gold inlay, gold beaded inset frames, sculpted brass corner ornaments, short hinge plates along the outer edges, and compact floral rosettes. Mirrored balanced composition with diffused studio lighting. No background surroundings, perspective angle, plain wood areas, door ring handles, letters, monograms, text, UI, people, or watermark. Flat usable two-door back texture atlas.
